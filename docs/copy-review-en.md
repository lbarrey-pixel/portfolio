- **Kind:** Quoting desk · B2B distributor
- **Title:** From a client's email to a finished proposal in the ERP
- **Summary:** The quoting team drops in a client's request and AI builds the proposal: items identified, products matched in the catalog, costs looked up, and a file ready for the ERP.
- **Flow:**
  - 01 · IN — Client email `React`
  - 02 · AI — AI extracts the items `LLMs`

- **Bullets:**

### Distribution center

## Method

- **Eyebrow:** Method
- **Title:** You know what you're getting before you pay for the build
- **Lead:** The same process on every project, from the first message to maintenance.
- **01 Written interview:** Straight questions, answered in your own time, no long meetings. You tell me how the business actually runs today.
- **02 Approved scenarios:** I write each behavior as a scenario, using your real data. You approve scenarios, not technical documents.
- **03 Built with tests:** Every scenario becomes an automated test. Nothing goes live until it passes all of them.
- **04 Documented handover:** Video manual, code and accounts in your name from day one. No hidden monthly fees.
- **05 Changes by ticket:** You request changes through a ticket. The agent prepares and tests them, and keeps the previous version in case you need to roll back.
